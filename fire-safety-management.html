The Challenge

Many smaller housing providers do not have a dedicated in-house fire safety specialist.

As regulatory expectations increase, this can create pressure around:

  • Oversight of FRA programmes
  • Quality and consistency of assessments
  • Action tracking and prioritisation
  • Contractor coordination
  • Evidence management
  • Governance reporting

Fire Clear provides structured, proportionate and defensible fire safety management support — either on an interim basis or as an ongoing fractional service.

Our Role

We act as your external fire safety lead.

Our focus is to ensure that fire safety is managed systematically, not reactively. This may include:

Stabilising an existing programme

Establishing structure where none currently exists

Strengthening governance oversight

Providing independent assurance to senior management

Core Service Areas

FRA Programme Management

+
  • Establishing risk-based inspection programmes
  • Coordinating assessors
  • Reviewing scope and methodology
  • Ensuring consistency and proportionality
  • Aligning assessment outputs with management capacity

We ensure the programme is manageable, prioritised and defensible.

Review and Validation of FRAs

+
  • Desktop technical review of completed FRAs
  • Identification of inconsistencies or over/under-scoping
  • Assessment of suitability and sufficiency
  • Assurance reporting for senior management

This provides confidence that your FRA programme can withstand scrutiny.

Action Management and Workstream Coordination

+
  • Curating and categorising FRA actions
  • Risk-based prioritisation
  • Assigning actions internally and to contractors
  • Tracking progress
  • Identifying bottlenecks and delivery risks

The aim is to convert assessment findings into structured delivery.

Project Management and Oversight

+
  • Oversight of fire safety works
  • Coordination of contractors
  • Programme tracking
  • Escalation where required

We provide structure and challenge to ensure delivery remains aligned with risk priorities.

Audit and Verification of Fire Safety Works

+
  • Sampling of completed works
  • Quality assurance checks
  • Evidence validation
  • Close-out verification

Ensuring that completion claims are supported by suitable evidence.

Compliance Evidence and Governance Reporting

+
  • Curating compliance documentation
  • Structuring audit trails
  • Preparing board-level reporting summaries
  • Supporting defensible decision-making

We help ensure that fire safety governance is clear, recorded and demonstrable.

Policy, Procedure and System Development

+
  • Fire safety policy drafting
  • Procedure development
  • Standard operating procedures
  • Role and responsibility frameworks
  • Competence mapping

The goal is long-term sustainability, not ongoing dependency.
