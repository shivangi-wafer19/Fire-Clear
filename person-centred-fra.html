Overview

Person-Centred Fire Risk Assessment (PC-FRA) provides a structured method for assessing and managing elevated fire risk associated with individual residents.

Particularly relevant in:

  • Specialised and supported housing
  • Extra care schemes
  • Temporary accommodation
  • General needs housing where vulnerability has been identified

AIM

To reduce risk proportionately while supporting lawful occupancy and resident wellbeing.

When PC-FRA Is Required

PC-FRA may be appropriate where one or more of the following apply:

A resident presents increased fire risk

Evidence of repeated fire-setting or unsafe behaviour

Health conditions affecting judgement or mobility

Standard control measures are insufficient

Evacuation strategy requires adaptation

Multi-agency input is needed

The process is risk-based and tailored to the individual circumstances.

The Process

Our structured approach includes:

1

Review of relevant building information and evacuation strategy

2

Engagement with housing management and (where appropriate) care providers

3

Consideration of resident-specific risk factors

4

Assessment of ignition sources and behavioural risk

5

Evaluation of fire protection measures

6

Identification of proportionate control measures

7

Documentation of rationale and agreed actions

The outcome is a clear, defensible record of assessment and decision-making.

Proportionate Risk Management

PC-FRA is not about exclusion or blanket restrictions.

It seeks to:

  • Balance safety and independence
  • Identify realistic control measures
  • Clarify responsibilities across teams
  • Record justification for decisions
  • Support consistent management

RECOMMENDATIONS MAY INCLUDE:

Environmental controls

Equipment adjustments

Supervision arrangements

Detection upgrades

Review of evacuation planning

Governance and Legal Context

Person-centred assessments must align with:

Clear documentation is essential to demonstrate that decisions are proportionate, considered and lawful.

Fire Safety Legislation

Compliance with applicable statutory requirements

Equality Considerations

Awareness of duties to residents with protected characteristics

Housing Law

Alignment with tenancy and landlord obligations

Safeguarding Principles

Integration with safeguarding frameworks

Organisational Policy

Consistency with internal governance standards

Training and Internal Capability

We offer structured training for housing officers and managers to support internal PC-FRA processes.

TRAINING CAN INCLUDE:

  • Understanding when PC-FRA is appropriate

  • Legal and governance considerations

  • Risk assessment methodology

  • Recording and documentation standards

  • Integration with existing fire safety management systems

The objective is to build confidence and consistency within your organisation — not to self-deliver, but to strengthen capability, understanding and informed decision-making.

Integration with Fire Safety Management

PC-FRA should not sit in isolation.
It should integrate with:

Building-wide Fire Risk Findings

Evacuation Strategy

Action Tracking System

Governance Reporting

Safeguarding Frameworks

We ensure that person-centred risk management aligns with wider fire safety systems.
